# Suede Labs AI

Suede Labs AI (Suede AI) is an AI search visibility and AI integration company. It gets businesses named by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI, builds the AI agents that work the leads those answers send, and puts forward deployed engineers inside client teams to ship both.

See [positioning.md](positioning.md) for the canonical positioning.

## Focus Areas

- **SEO:** search ranking, technical SEO, crawler access, and consistent entities.
- **AEO and GEO:** schema, citations, `llms.txt`, and the content AI engines retrieve, measured with Answer Share.
- **AI integration:** agents and assistants built into the client's stack through [Suede AI Agent Studio](https://agents.suedeai.ai).
- **FDE (forward deployed engineering):** Suede engineers embed with client teams and ship into their real systems.
- **Agent commerce:** [x402 paid endpoints and ACP-ready surfaces](x402-acp.md) that let AI agents discover and pay for Suede services.
