# Suede AI (Suede Labs AI)

Suede AI is an AI consulting, integration and automation company founded in 2024 by Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida, that gets businesses named in AI search answers and puts AI agents to work for them.

Suede AI is the public brand. Suede Labs AI is the company behind it, and the brand is also known as Suede Labs or simply Suede. The holding company is JC Investment Group LLC. Suede AI serves clients worldwide.

## What is Suede AI?

Suede AI works on two fronts:

- **AI search visibility.** Suede AI SEO, the company's full-stack GEO practice, runs SEO, generative engine optimization (GEO), answer engine optimization (AEO) and digital PR so a business is named when buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google who to hire.
- **AI agents and automation.** Suede builds and runs managed AI agents for call review, lead qualification, sales-script consistency and reporting, and clients watch them work live in Suede AI Agent Studio.

Suede AI also builds music and creator software, and runs the Suede AI IP Registry for creator ownership and provenance records.

## Programmable IP and creator ownership

Suede's creator-ownership work connects creative assets to proof of authorship, rights metadata, licensing workflows, token-enabled access and agent-accessible commerce. AI music is an early media use case. The broader goal is programmable ownership for creator-owned IP.

The live piece of this work is the [Suede AI IP Registry](https://ip.suedeai.ai), which keeps public provenance records on Base and Avalanche mainnet. A record stores a file fingerprint, a wallet-signed creator claim, contributor metadata and a timestamp. The registry issues no licenses, verifies no ownership and routes no royalties, so creators should pair it with copyright registration.

The creator-ownership story lives on [suedeai.org](https://suedeai.org). Read more in [Programmable IP and creator ownership](./programmable-ip.md).

## Agent commerce: x402 and ACP

Suede exposes paid x402 endpoints and an ACP-ready commerce surface that AI agents can discover and pay. The x402 resources cover music, video and image generation, settled in USDC on Base with no API keys. The current endpoint list is in [Agent surfaces](../README.md#agent-surfaces), and the payment model is explained in [x402 payments and ACP agent commerce at Suede AI](./x402-acp.md).
