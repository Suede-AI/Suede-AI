# Suede AI Positioning

This is the canonical positioning for Suede AI (Suede Labs AI). The README, every doc in this repo, and all Suede copy should match it.

## One line

**Get named by AI search. Put agents to work.**

## What Suede AI is

Suede AI is an AI search visibility and AI integration company. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI who to hire, and they call whoever gets named. Suede gets businesses named in that answer, builds the AI agents that work the leads it sends, and puts forward deployed engineers inside client teams to ship both.

## Focus areas

| Area | What it means |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Search ranking, technical SEO, crawler access, and consistent entities across the web |
| **AEO and GEO** | Answer engine and generative engine optimization: schema, citations, `llms.txt`, and the content AI engines retrieve, so a business is named and cited correctly by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews |
| **AI integration** | AI agents and assistants built into the client's own stack for calls, lead qualification, follow-up, support and reporting, through [Suede AI Agent Studio](https://agents.suedeai.ai) |
| **FDE (forward deployed engineering)** | Suede engineers embed with the client team and ship SEO, GEO and AI integrations into the client's real systems, CMS, data and workflows, instead of handing over a deck |

**Agent commerce** supports all four. [x402 paid endpoints and ACP-ready surfaces](x402-acp.md) let AI agents discover and pay for Suede services.

## Proof

- **Answer Share** measures how often a business is named across AI engines on the prompts its buyers ask. It is baselined before the work and measured again after, with dated screenshots.
- **Suede is its own case study.** Suede's own visibility in Google Search and AI answers comes from the same in-house SEO and GEO process it sells.

## Writing rules

- Lead with SEO, GEO, AI integration and FDE. Creator ownership, programmable IP, music products and the SUEDE token are part of Suede's history and portfolio, not the lead.
- Use the same entity names everywhere: **Suede AI** (also **Suede Labs AI**), founded and led by **Jason Colapietro**, also known publicly as **Jay Colapietro** and **Johnny Suede**.
- Use specific promises over vague ones. Say "Get named by ChatGPT and Perplexity on the prompts your buyers ask," not "Grow your AI presence."
- Don't make up results, client names or numbers. Use only Answer Share data and screenshots that exist.
