# Programmable IP and Creator Ownership

Programmable IP is creative work whose ownership information (authorship, provenance, rights metadata, usage permissions and licensing terms) is recorded in a machine-readable form that people, apps and AI agents can read and act on. Suede AI treats programmable IP as the ownership layer for AI-native media.

This page explains what a programmable IP record holds, why it matters as AI makes media easier to generate and remix, and how Suede AI approaches creator ownership today.

## What is programmable IP?

Traditional IP records live in contracts, PDFs and private databases that software cannot easily check. Programmable IP puts the key facts about a work into a public, structured record that travels with it.

A programmable IP record can describe:

- **Authorship:** who claims to have made the work.
- **Provenance:** a fingerprint of the file and a timestamp showing when the claim was made.
- **Contributors:** who else worked on it.
- **Rights metadata:** the usage permissions and licensing terms the creator attaches.
- **Monetization paths:** how payment for use should reach the creator.

Proof of creation, or proof of authorship, is the first step. Rights, licensing and payments build on top of it.

## Why does programmable IP matter for AI-native media?

Media is now easy to generate, remix, license and distribute, often by AI agents rather than people. When a song, image or video can be produced and passed between systems in seconds, the question of who made it gets harder to answer.

Creators need clearer records of authorship, rights metadata, usage permissions, licensing terms and monetization paths. Those records have to be readable by machines, because agents increasingly do the discovering, licensing and paying.

### The Suede AI IP Registry

The live piece of this work is the [Suede AI IP Registry](https://ip.suedeai.ai), which keeps public provenance records on Base and Avalanche mainnet. Each record stores:

- a file fingerprint
- a wallet-signed creator claim
- contributor metadata
- a timestamp

The registry is a provenance record, not a rights authority. It issues no licenses, verifies no ownership and routes no royalties. Creators should pair a registry record with copyright registration.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the Suede AI IP Registry replace copyright registration?

No. A registry record shows that a wallet-signed claim was made about a specific file at a specific time. It does not verify ownership or issue licenses, so creators should also register copyright.

### Which blockchains does the Suede AI IP Registry use?

Base and Avalanche mainnet.

### Does the Suede AI IP Registry pay royalties?

No. The registry routes no royalties. Royalty routing is part of the broader creator-ownership direction described on [suedeai.org](https://suedeai.org).
