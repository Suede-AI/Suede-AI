## 2026-10-10 — AI-guided digital products (Hussain Ibarra)

- Source: https://x.com/HussainIbarra/status/2108563595676299429
- Article: "How to build a profitable digital product (in 7 steps)" by Hussain Ibarra, posted 2026-10-09

**Summary:** One-person businesses can now sell digital products with AI built in. Courses are static, but an AI trained on the creator's knowledge, frameworks, and voice can guide each buyer through the material. That raises the product's perceived value and gets buyers better results.

**The 7 steps:**

1. **Sell your sawdust.** Package the systems you already use, like a producer's drum kit, an editor's presets, or a writer's process.
2. **Define a Dream Customer.** Pick one specific customer avatar, not everyone.
3. **Write a specific promise.** "Helping [WHO] go from [HELL] to [HEAVEN] so they can [BENEFIT]."
4. **Build the curriculum.** 3–5 milestones become modules, and 3–5 action steps per milestone become lessons. Add an AI tutor so buyers can get feedback.
5. **Become your own case study.** Your own results count as proof.
6. **Write the sales page.** Promise, problem, process, features and benefits, proof, price, guarantee, CTA, FAQ.
7. **Send traffic.** Promote more often and post more content. More posts mean promos are a smaller share of the feed.

**Relevance to Suede:**

- **Producer kits as programmable IP.** Drum kits, presets, and stems are "sawdust" products. Suede could mint them with provenance, licensing terms, and token-gated access.
- **AI-guided creator products.** Let creators attach an AI version of themselves, trained on their own frameworks, to a kit or course. Examples: a production coach that ships with a sample pack, or a mixing tutor with a preset bundle.
- **Agent-accessible commerce.** These products could be sold to buyers and AI agents through x402 and ACP checkout flows.
- **Content strategy.** The promotion-frequency point fits the $SUEDE content plan: post more often so CTAs are a small share of the feed.
